支持哪些 Mac？Which Macs are supported?

Orbit 需要 macOS 14 或更新版本。正式安装包开放后，这一页会明确标注支持 Apple 芯片、Intel，或两者通用。Orbit requires macOS 14 or later. Each published package will explicitly list Apple silicon, Intel, or Universal support.

旧版本怎么更新？How do I update?

在 Orbit 的「关于」中点击「检查更新」。下载后退出 Orbit，把新版拖入「应用程序」替换旧版。配置与授权独立保存，替换 App 不会清除它们。Choose Check for Updates in Orbit’s About settings. Quit Orbit, then replace the old app in Applications. Your configuration and license are stored separately.

试用到期后会怎么样？What happens after the trial?

基础轮盘仍可使用。子轮盘、多主轮盘和脚本等授权功能，需要使用 CDK 永久激活后继续使用；不会自动扣款。Core wheel features remain available. Sub-wheels, multiple wheels and scripts require lifetime activation after the trial. There is no automatic billing.
